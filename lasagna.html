Lasagna

Ingredients Meat Sauce:
    1 lb (450g) ground beef or a mix of ground beef and Italian sausage 1 yellow onion, diced 2 cloves garlic, minced 36 oz pasta or marinara sauce 2 tbsp tomato paste 1 tsp Italian seasoning Salt and black pepper (to taste)
Cheese Mixture:
    12 lasagna noodles 2 cups ricotta or cottage cheese 1 large egg 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped 3.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided) ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
Instructions
    Cook the Noodles: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the lasagna noodles al dente according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  1. Make the Meat Sauce: In a large skillet or pot, brown the ground beef (and sausage, if using) with the diced onion and minced garlic over medium-high heat until no longer pink. Drain excess grease. Stir in the pasta sauce, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 5–10 minutes.
  2. Mix the Cheeses: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta (or cottage cheese), egg, chopped parsley, half of the Parmesan cheese, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. Assemble the Lasagna:
    1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
    2. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    3. Layer 3 to 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce.
    4. Spread a portion of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles, then sprinkle with mozzarella.
    5. Spoon a layer of meat sauce over the cheese.
    6. Repeat the layers (noodles, cheese mixture, meat sauce, mozzarella) until you finish with a top layer of noodles covered in remaining meat sauce and a generous blanket of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  4. Bake: Cover the dish with foil and bake at 350°F for 45 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until bubbly and golden brown on top.
  5. Rest and Serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
